A new kind of management company

Skyfire is committed to guiding, developing and inspiring artists to ignite their talents to their fullest potential, with career consulting, development, branding, social media strategy and new avenues for artists at every level.

At its core is a mission to create opportunities and visibility for underrepresented groups in entertainment.

Ryan M. Luévano

Owner and talent manager

Ryan is a Latinx, award-winning playwright, director and screenwriter. Before founding Skyfire, he was a manager at Mattie Management under Mattie Webb, working with clients including Frankie McNellis, Leah McSweeney, Ron Song and Fabian Alomar.

He has taught music at Woodbury University, Santa Ana College and Rio Hondo College, teaches at A Noise Within Theatre Company, serves on the board of the Foundation for New American Musicals and is a member of the Dramatists Guild.
