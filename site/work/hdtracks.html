HDtracks

HDtracks is the world’s leading high-resolution music store. HDtracks underwent a complete rebranding and redesign of its website in 2019. The redesign included building a new website from the ground up, establishing database storage, UX/UI design, updated branding, logos, and ad campaigns.

The rebranding and redesign resulted in an increase in customer retention and sales, making HDtracks the most user-friendly Hi-Res download music store.
