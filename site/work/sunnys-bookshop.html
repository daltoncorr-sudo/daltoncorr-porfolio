Sunny's Bookshop

Creative Direction, Rebrand and Logo Design

A full rebrand and a hand-drawn logo for an independent bookshop, ahead of its second store.

Client
Sunny's Bookshop, Los Angeles
Year
2026
Role
Creative Director, full rebrand and logo design

Sunny's Bookshop is an independent bookshop and book bar in Tarzana, with a second shop coming to the NoHo Arts District. I was the creative director on the full rebrand and the logo, all of it built for the new store. The owner, Sanaz Tamjidi, already had a brand people loved: a seal, a cursive signature, a dark green. She asked for it refined, not reinvented. So I drew the open book by hand, rebuilt the signature to be read rather than admired, set an SB monogram inside the seal's two rings, paired a serif with the script, and wrote the rules into a 24-page bible. The slogan was already stitched into her label: For readers and writers.

Outcome

Delivered in October 2026: every mark in green, vellum, black and white, as SVG, PDF, EPS, AI and PNG, with the fonts, colour references down to Pantone, thread and foil, and the bible to keep it all consistent. The identity is ready for the NoHo opening in 2027.
