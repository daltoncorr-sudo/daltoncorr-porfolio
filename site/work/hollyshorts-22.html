22nd Annual HollyShorts Film Festival

Art Director

TCL Chinese Theatres — Hollywood, August 2026

Art direction for the 22nd Annual Oscar®-Qualifying HollyShorts Film Festival, August 13–23, 2026, at the TCL Chinese Theatres in Hollywood.

The official poster is a hand-painted festival night: a red carpet through a packed house to the screen, the Chinese Theatre on one side and the Ovation Hollywood arch on the other. The figures dancing on that screen became the festival’s system, one on every badge, and the 22 is drawn in film strip.

Work includes: official poster, festival logo, badge design, merch design, wine label, production design across the TCL Chinese Theatres and Ovation Hollywood (step-and-repeats, tower signs, marquee, courtyard and directory screens, DJ booth), on-screen creative, and social media assets.

Poster

Hand-painted official poster: the red carpet, the Chinese Theatre and the Hollywood sign, with the festival’s dancers on the screen

Logo

The 22, drawn in film strip

Badges

8 badge designs, one figure for every pass — drag to explore, tap the front badge to turn it over

Production Design

TCL Chinese Theatres and Ovation Hollywood — marquee, step-and-repeats, tower signs, courtyard and directory screens, DJ booth, on-screen creative

Merch

Tees, hoodies, totes and hats from the poster and its figures, and the festival wine label
