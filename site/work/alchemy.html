Alchemy

Composer, Music Supervisor

Director Remington Long — New York City — 2019

Composed the original score and served as music supervisor for Alchemy, a documentary about Bonnie Ohara, the founder of a one-woman bakery in Modesto, California. Directed by Remington Long.

Winner of over ten awards including Sundance Collab, Paris Short Film Festival, Greenwich Village Film Festival (Best Documentary), Napa Valley Film Festival, and Hamptons Film Festival.
