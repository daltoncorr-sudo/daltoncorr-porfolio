Espresso Tempo

Founder, Composer & Designer

Music Production Studio, 2020

I founded Espresso Tempo, a music production studio, in 2020. It has released 11 albums. I composed all of the music and designed all of the branding: the cup-and-saucer mark, the covers, the liner notes, the ads and the merch.

EP 7 includes “Interactive,” the theme song for The Future Is Ow. EP 11 is the soundtrack to Into The Ether With Kid Nicoleman.
