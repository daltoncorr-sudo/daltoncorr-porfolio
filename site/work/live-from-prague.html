Live from Prague

Composer & Pianist

Palffy Palace — Prague, 2014

Original songs "Modi" and "Skylight Blues" composed and arranged by Dalton Corr, recorded live at the Palffy Palace in Prague. The recordings later served as theme music for the NYU Prague Campus podcast. Band member Peter Anspach went on to play in Goose.

Dalton Corr (piano), John Marcus (bass), Peter Anspach (drums), Raymundo Gutierrez (flute).
