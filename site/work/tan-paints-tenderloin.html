Tan Paints the Tenderloin

Composer

Director Matthew Bunker — San Francisco — 2022

Original score for the documentary short Tan Paints the Tenderloin, directed by Matthew Bunker. The film follows Tan Sirinumas, a Thai immigrant living in San Francisco who finally receives recognition for his painting.

Official Selection, SF Shorts Film Festival 2020.
