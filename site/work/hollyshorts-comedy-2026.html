10th Annual HollyShorts Comedy Film Festival

Art Director

LOOK Dine-In Cinemas — Glendale, May 2026

Art direction for the 10th anniversary of HollyShorts Comedy, May 1–3, 2026, at LOOK Dine-In Cinemas in Glendale, California.

Prestige but silly: the anniversary poster builds one isometric set of comedy props on hot pink, from a TAKE 10 clapperboard and a director’s chair to a rubber duck, popcorn and banana peels, and its inflated, candy-like type carries through the badges, the official selections and the award show.

Work includes: official poster and animated poster, 10th anniversary logo, badge design, official selection and screening schedule campaigns, the IMGN film fund competition, opening night invitation, award show design, winner laurels, step-and-repeat, merch design, and social media assets.
