© 2026 Dalton Corr

Dalton Corr is an artist based in Los Angeles and New York City working across film, music, and visual arts. His visual work includes art directing Oscar- and BAFTA-qualifying film festivals in Hollywood, London, and Dubai, theatrical poster designs showcased at events such as the Cannes Film Festival, brand direction, photoshoots, web design, and original paintings. As a composer and producer he has scored feature films, released studio albums, and has toured with bands internationally at events at SXSW and Pálffy Palace in Prague. He is the founder of the music house Espresso Tempo and its design counterpart, Opmet Osserpse. His collaborators include Spike Lee, Reece Feldman (guywithamoviecamera), Grammy-winning artists David and Norman Chesky, NYU’s Village Records, Manhattan gallery Chop Suey Club, and fashion brand Weissman’s.

Dalton studied music at New York University and the Westminster Conservatory of Music in Princeton, and is a member of the American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers (ASCAP) and the Society of Composers & Lyricists (SCL).
