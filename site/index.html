Dalton Corr is a designer, composer, and art director working across film, music, and visual arts between New York and Los Angeles. He art directs Oscar- and BAFTA-qualifying film festivals in Hollywood, London, and Dubai, and has designed theatrical posters shown at the Cannes Film Festival. As a composer and producer he scores feature films, releases studio albums, and has performed at SXSW and Pälffy Palace. He is the founder of the music house Espresso Tempo and its design counterpart, Opmet Osserpse. His collaborators include Spike Lee, Reece Feldman (guywithamoviecamera), Grammy-winning artists David and Norman Chesky, NYU’s Village Records, Manhattan gallery Chop Suey Club, and fashion brand Weissman’s.

He studied at New York University and the Westminster Conservatory of Music in Princeton, and is a member of the American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers (ASCAP) and the Society of Composers & Lyricists (SCL).
