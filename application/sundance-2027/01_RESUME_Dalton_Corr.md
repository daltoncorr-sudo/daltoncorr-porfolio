## PROFILE

Art director and designer with a decade of film-festival and entertainment design experience,
including four consecutive years leading the complete visual identity of the Oscar-qualifying
HollyShorts Film Festival — from campaign concept and poster through environmental graphics,
signage, badges, awards, motion, social, merchandise, and the festival's official website. Led
the creative launch of the festival's first international edition (HollyShorts London, 2025),
building a city-specific identity inside an established parent brand. Poster and campaign work
named among the world's best festival posters two years running (FestivalsInLA, 2023 & 2024),
with festival campaigns covered by Variety, Deadline, Daily Mail, and TimeOut. Also a working
film composer (Sundance Collab, 2019), bringing a filmmaker's perspective to festival design.
