# Cover Letter — Festival Lead Graphic Designer

*(Draft. Addressee: Sundance Institute hiring team; if the posting names the Creative Director,

Dear Sundance Institute team,

In 2025 I led the creative launch of an established film festival's first edition in a new city —
the inaugural HollyShorts London. The challenge was the one Sundance now faces in Boulder: honor
an identity audiences already love, while building a visual language that belongs to a new place.
I'd like to bring exactly that experience to the 2027 Sundance Film Festival as your Festival
Lead Graphic Designer.

For the past four years I have owned the complete visual identity of the Oscar-qualifying
HollyShorts Film Festival, moving from designer (2022) to art director of the 19th, 20th, and
21st editions. That means the full arc this role asks for: concepting the campaign narrative
each year, designing the official poster and key art, then carrying that language through every
surface a festival touches — billboards and step-and-repeats, event signage and photo walls at
TCL Chinese Theatres and the Egyptian Theatre, on-screen and motion graphics, badge and award
systems, social and digital campaigns, merchandise, and the festival's official website, which I
also designed and built. My posters for HollyShorts were named among the world's best film
festival posters by FestivalsInLA in both 2023 and 2024, and the campaigns I art directed were
covered by Variety, Deadline, Daily Mail, and TimeOut.

I also know what it takes to lead design at festival tempo: multiple venues installing
simultaneously, print vendors and fabricators on fixed deadlines, sponsors with their own
requirements, and an award show going live at the end of it. In 2025 I additionally led the
rebrand of the Hollywood Comedy Shorts Film Festival into HollyShorts Comedy — new logo, palette,
typography, and a full design system across print, digital, and event assets, including
interactive AR activations. And at Cannes in 2023 and 2024, I created original illustration and
poster work for Kodak-hosted events.

Sundance is personal for me. I'm a working film composer as well as a designer — my score and
music supervision for the documentary *Alchemy* was part of Sundance Collab in 2019, and I
recently completed my first feature score. I design for festivals as someone who has sat in
those theaters as a filmmaker, and I understand that Festival graphics aren't decoration — they
are the first frame of every filmmaker's premiere.

I'm based between Los Angeles and New York — both listed locations for this role — and I'm fully
available for the September 2, 2026 through January 26, 2027 engagement, including travel to
Boulder and the extended hours a festival demands. Boulder's first Sundance deserves an identity
audiences will remember; I've helped a festival plant its flag in a new city before, and I would
be honored to help Sundance do the same.

Thank you for your consideration.

Dalton Corr
